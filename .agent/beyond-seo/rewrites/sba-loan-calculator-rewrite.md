    <h2>How SBA Loan Payments and Upfront Guaranty Fees Are Calculated</h2>
    <p>
      Unlike conventional business financing, the SBA assesses an upfront **Guaranty Fee** on the portion of the loan guaranteed by the federal government. Most business owners choose to roll this fee into the total loan amount.
    </p>

    <h3>SBA 7(a) Upfront Guaranty Fee Structure (Current 2026 Tiers):</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Loans of $150,000 or Less:</strong> 0% upfront guaranty fee (with 0% annual ongoing lender servicing fee).</li>
      <li><strong>Loans of $150,001 to $700,000:</strong> 3.0% on the guaranteed portion (the SBA typically guarantees 75% to 85%).</li>
      <li><strong>Loans of $700,001 to $5,000,000:</strong> 3.5% on the guaranteed portion up to $1,000,000, plus 3.75% on the guaranteed portion exceeding $1,000,000.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>
      <strong>Monthly Payment Calculation Formula:</strong><br>
      The standard monthly amortization equation applied to SBA principal and interest payments is:
    </p>
    <div class="code-formula-block">
      <code>M = P * [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n – 1 ]</code>
    </div>
    <p>
      Where <strong>M</strong> is monthly payment, <strong>P</strong> is total financed balance (including rolled guaranty fees), <strong>r</strong> is the periodic monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12), and <strong>n</strong> is the total number of monthly installments (e.g. 120 for 10 years, 300 for 25 years).
    </p>
  </section>
