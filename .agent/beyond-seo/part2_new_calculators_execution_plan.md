## 🏛️ AEO & GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Architecture

Every new calculator incorporates the **Beyond SEO AEO Framework** to secure Google AI Overviews, Perplexity citations, and ChatGPT Search results:

1. **Direct Answer Block:** A concise, 40- to 60-word bolded paragraph positioned directly below the tool output.
2. **Tabular Comparison Data:** Structured Markdown tables benchmarked with current 2026 interest rates, tax brackets, and lending limits.
3. **Structured Entity Schema:** Full dual `@graph` JSON-LD implementing `WebApplication` and `FAQPage` with accepted answers.
4. **Authoritative Government & Institutional Sourcing:** Citations to CFPB, IRS, HUD, Fannie Mae, and SBA.
5. **AI Crawler Discovery Manifest:** Deployed at the root domain level via [`/llms.txt`](file:///c:/Users/COMTECH%2003122811806/Downloads/Beyond%20SEO%20%20Your%20AI-Powered%20SEO%20Agency%20in%20One%20Skill/beyond-seo/llms.txt) following the official LLMs.txt specification.
