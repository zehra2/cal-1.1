💡 Quick Answer: How is Property Tax Calculated?

Property taxes are assessed locally using the millage formula: Annual Tax = (Taxable Assessed Value × Millage Rate) ÷ 1,000. Effective property tax rates range from a low of 0.32% in Hawaii to 2.23% in New Jersey. In states like Texas, high property taxes offset the lack of a state personal income tax.
