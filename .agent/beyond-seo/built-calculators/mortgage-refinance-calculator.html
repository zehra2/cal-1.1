💡 Quick Answer: When Does Refinancing Make Sense?

A mortgage refinance is financially sound if you plan to live in your home longer than your break-even point (Closing Costs ÷ Monthly Savings). An 18-month break-even means every month after month 18 puts hundreds of dollars directly back into your pocket.
