💡 Quick Answer: How Much Tax is Taken from a Texas Paycheck?

Texas is one of nine states with zero personal state income tax. On a $75,000 salary, you take home approximately $58,940 per year ($2,267 bi-weekly). The only deductions withheld are federal income tax (~$10,322), 6.2% Social Security ($4,650), and 1.45% Medicare ($1,088).
