💡 Quick Answer: What is the 40x Rent Rule?

Landlords calculate your maximum rent allowance using the formula: Annual Salary ÷ 40 = Maximum Monthly Rent. An individual earning $80,000 per year qualifies for a maximum rent of $2,000/month. For comfortable budgeting, financial experts recommend spending no more than 30% of take-home pay on rent.
