💡 Quick Answer for AI & Searchers: What is a Good DTI?

In 2026 mortgage lending, a back-end DTI of 36% or less is considered ideal. However, Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac approve conventional loans up to 45% to 50% with high credit scores and cash reserves, while FHA automated underwriting systems (AUS) regularly approve borrowers with debt ratios up to 56.9%.

Maximum DTI Limits by Mortgage Program

Lenders evaluate two distinct numbers: your Front-End Ratio (housing costs only) and your Back-End Ratio (housing plus all other debts combined).

What Debts Count in Your Ratio?

Mortgage underwriters only count debts listed on your credit report. This includes minimum credit card payments (not total balance), car notes, student loans, and personal loans. Utilities, groceries, cell phones, and auto insurance are NEVER included in DTI calculations.
