💡 Quick Answer: What Are Typical Boat Loan Terms in 2026?

Boat loans range from 5 to 20 years. On a $65,000 boat with 15% down ($9,750), a 15-year loan at 7.74% APR results in a monthly payment of $519/month. Most marine lenders require a 10% to 20% down payment and a licensed marine survey for pre-owned vessels over 20 feet.
