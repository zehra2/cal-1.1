💡 Quick Answer: How Much Do HOA Fees Reduce Affordability?

Under standard 30-year mortgage underwriting at 6.5% interest, every $100 per month in HOA dues reduces your mortgage loan qualification by roughly $15,800. A $350 monthly HOA fee reduces your purchasing power by more than $55,000 compared to a home with no HOA dues.

Why Lenders Care About HOA Fees

Mortgage lenders add 100% of your monthly HOA dues directly into your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio. Because the dues are a mandatory legal encumbrance on the property deed, failing to pay them can result in an association lien or foreclosure, making it a primary risk factor in underwriting.
