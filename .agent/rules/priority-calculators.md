## De-prioritized
Academic, generic math, or novelty tools. These should be:
- `noindex`-tagged, and/or
- Siloed under `/tools/` (kept off the main SEO clusters entirely)

## Cluster logic
Each calculator belongs to a content cluster and must link within it:
- **Loan cluster** (`/loan-calculators/`): SBA loan, SBA loan payment,
  business loan, land loan, RV loan, auto loan, personal loan
- **Real estate cluster** (`/real-estate/`): cap rate, and future
  mortgage/rental tools
- **Business cluster** (`/business/`): markup, profit margin, freelance rate

When building any calculator, always link to at least 3 others in its
cluster, plus 1 pillar/guide page for that cluster.
