## Data interpretation notes

- The Chart sheet is the best source for the 7-day total: 5 clicks, 4,273 impressions, 0.12% CTR, and weighted average position about 68.5. Dimension tables do not fully reconcile: the query table totals 3 clicks/3,174 impressions, while the page table totals 5 clicks/4,417 impressions. Treat dimension tables as directional and use the Chart sheet for the headline total.
- The highest visible demand is around closing-cost tools: the closing-cost page has 711 impressions at position 79.34, and several closing-cost queries together have substantial impressions but positions mostly 72–80. This indicates a ranking/relevance opportunity, not a title-only CTR problem.
- The SBA page has 460 impressions at position 77.95 and the RV loan page 391 impressions at position 72.66. These are not page-one CTR opportunities yet; they require stronger topical relevance, internal links, and possibly clearer intent sections before snippet testing.
- Search Console shows off-sitemap or redirected/off-brand URLs receiving impressions, including a page that redirects to the homepage and a legacy investment URL redirecting to the investment-growth calculator. Audit indexation and sitemap consistency before publishing more pages.
- Country data is extremely small: Pakistan has 3 of 5 clicks, while the United States has 1 click from 3,717 impressions. Do not infer that Pakistan is the target market; use a longer date range and confirm the property/country filters.
