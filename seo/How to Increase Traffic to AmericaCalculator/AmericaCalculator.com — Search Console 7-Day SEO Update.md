## 1. Executive Summary

**The Search Console data confirms that AmericaCalculator has begun to earn search impressions, but it does not yet rank strongly enough for those impressions to become meaningful traffic.** From 6–12 August, the site recorded **5 clicks from 4,273 impressions**, a **0.12% CTR**, and an impression-weighted average position of **68.5** [1]. This is primarily a visibility problem, not a snippet problem: the highest-impression pages—Closing Cost Calculator (711 impressions, position 79.34), SBA Loan Calculator (460, 77.95), RV Loan Calculator (391, 72.66), Home Addition Cost Calculator (388, 61.47), and Interest-Only Loan Calculator (348, 82.78)—are normally far below page one [1]. Closing-cost queries alone account for at least 401 visible impressions across seven variants, yet rank mainly between positions 72 and 80. The strongest immediate work is to restore the broken Construction hub, rebuild internal topic paths, and improve the content/methodology/source coverage of closing cost, SBA, and home-addition pages [2] [3]. The data also exposes legacy/off-topic URLs in Search Console that redirect to the homepage; these should be cleaned up rather than treated as traffic wins. The site has an indexation footprint; it now needs relevance, trust, structure, and time to earn higher positions.

## 3. Where Google Is Showing the Site

### The main demand signal is closing costs

The visible closing-cost query cluster has at least **401 impressions** across the following variants: “closing cost calculator,” “closing cost calculator estimator,” “calculate closing costs,” “estimate closing costs,” “estimated closing costs,” “closing costs calculator,” and “closing costs calculator for buyer.” All showed zero clicks and average positions roughly between 72 and 89 [1]. The corresponding Closing Cost Calculator page generated **711 impressions**, no clicks, and position **79.34** [1].

This is valuable evidence: Google recognizes that the page may be related to closing-cost intent, but it does not yet consider it competitive enough to rank where searchers can see it. Changing a meta description alone will not solve a position-79 problem. The page should become the site’s definitive buyer closing-cost resource, with transparent methodology, dated source citations, and useful decision content around cash to close, loan types, prepaids, seller concessions, and state/local variation [3].

### SBA and lending pages have demand, but weak visibility

The SBA Loan Calculator has **460 impressions**, no clicks, and position **77.95**; the visible SBA-related query set includes “sba 504 loan calculator,” “sba 504 loan amortization schedule,” “sba loan payment calculator,” “sba loan calculator,” and “504 loan calculator” [1]. The page is already content-rich, but the earlier audit found one conflicting payment figure in its formula example, and the page combines several loan intents [4]. Correct the calculation discrepancy first, strengthen official citations and update control, then decide whether a dedicated, source-backed SBA 504 page is needed to match the specific 504 demand.

### Home Addition Cost is a practical construction opportunity

The Home Addition Cost Calculator received **388 impressions**, zero clicks, and position **61.47**. The query “home addition cost calculator” is relatively closer at position **44.41** on 29 impressions, with further demand from related variants [1]. The page already has useful type, size, quality, region, and build-direction inputs plus an itemised result [5]. Improve its traceable cost-source methodology, add current regional examples, and rebuild the broken Construction hub so related pages can reinforce it [2] [5].

## 4. Branded vs Non-Branded Search

The partial Queries table shows one brand query, “america calculator,” with **1 click from 12 impressions** and a position of **4.58**. In the same table, non-branded queries account for 2 clicks and 3,162 impressions [1]. Because this query table is capped/incomplete, those figures are directional rather than a complete brand split.

The strategic message is still clear: **brand search is not the growth engine.** The site must win non-branded calculator queries, but the current average positions show that it has not yet built enough relevance and authority in its highest-opportunity clusters. Do not expect title rewrites alone to create traffic while most demand sits beyond position 50.

## 6. Architecture and Indexation Findings Confirmed by Search Console

Search Console shows impressions for legacy or off-strategy URLs outside the 72-URL sitemap, including `calculator-bacalaureat.php`, `terminus.php`, `dave-ramsey-investment.php`, `ap-lang-score.php`, and `blox-fruits.php` [1]. Live checks confirm that `calculator-bacalaureat.php` and `terminus.php` issue a 301 to the homepage, while `dave-ramsey-investment.php` issues a 301 to the Investment Growth Calculator [6].

A 301 is appropriate only where the old page has a close, user-helpful replacement. Redirecting irrelevant former pages to the homepage is unlikely to strengthen the site’s intended calculator clusters and can leave confusing signals in Search Console. Keep valid 301s to closely matched replacements; for genuinely discontinued/off-brand pages with no equivalent, use the appropriate removal strategy and keep them out of the sitemap. The earlier crawl also found the linked `/construction/` hub returning 404; restoring it remains the first technical fix [2] [3].

## 8. What Not to Do

Do not publish dozens of unrelated calculators just to increase URL count. The data already shows that Google is testing multiple relevant pages but ranking them low. Fix topic depth, accuracy, internal links, source transparency, and hub structure first. Do not buy bulk backlinks or redirect irrelevant pages to the homepage. Do not call the current CTR a title-tag failure when average ranking is around position 68; ranking improvement must come before serious snippet testing.
